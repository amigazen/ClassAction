## Source release

Sources for ClassAction 2.83 and 3.6 were released on Aminet by Salim Gasmi
([util/wb/CASrc](https://aminet.net/package/util/wb/CASrc)) after he left
the Amiga platform, so they would remain available and might be useful to
others.

Sources for ClassAction 4 were uploaded to Aminet as
[util/wb/Classaction4_src](https://aminet.net/package/util/wb/Classaction4_src)
by Sylvio K. after Martin Elsner sold him his Amiga. The upload was made to
archive Elsner's work and in the hope that someone would continue the
project and possibly port the file manager to other platforms.

This repository preserves that source for continued development. Copyright
notices should be retained.
